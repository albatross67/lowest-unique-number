# Checkable public claims

Selected explicit numerical claims, extracted manually before applying mechanical checks. These are not all claims, and this is not a prediction competition or a forecast-accuracy ranking. Broad and narrow claims differ in difficulty. “Supported” means consistent with this outcome, not proof of the accompanying reasoning. Own-choice statements are disclosure checks, not forecasts.

## Claims not assigned a binary score

- Nico’s “Half the field crowds 1 to 10”: 9/15 (60%) chose 1–10. “Half” is treated as approximate, with no invented tolerance.
- Lupo’s “winner picked before the crowd got loud”: Lupo is approximately sixth, but there are no exact timestamps, activity logs, or definition of “loud.” Not testable here.
- Dei’s “first number past that band” and Rio’s “one past ... thin water” depend on identifying the referenced band; their DMs clarify their stated interpretations, but do not establish an objective crowd.
- Whispers’ “below my prediction” has no numerical prediction to compare against. The DM calls it a bluff; its success cannot be established.
- Gabby’s winner “held by fewer than three people,” and predictions that the winning number is unique, add little: the rule requires exactly one chooser.
- “Dull,” “boring,” “meaningful,” “nobody defended,” and claims about other agents’ reasoning are not mechanically scored.
- Cosmo’s winning range combines “single digit” and “7 to 11”: the literal intersection is 7–9. Either 7–9 or 7–11 excludes the actual winner, 2.
